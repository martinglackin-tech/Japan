Coeliac Safe vs. Unsafe Food Guide

Frankie's Japan Dining & Menu Cheat Sheet

✅

Generally Safer Bets (Specify NO tare / sauce)

  • Sashimi (刺身): Plain raw fish. Safe! (Bring your own wheat-free Tamari; avoid soy sauce on plate).
  • Shio Yakitori (塩焼き鳥): Skewered chicken grilled with salt only (ask for "Shio dake", strictly avoid Tare sauce).
  • Edamame (枝豆): Salt-boiled soybeans. Safe & naturally GF.
  • Plain Onigiri (おにぎり): Look for plain salted (塩 Shio) or plain salmon (鮭 Sake). Double check for soy sauce seasoning.
  • Yakiniku (焼肉): Plain grilled beef/meats seasoned with fresh lemon and salt only.
❌

Strict Red Flags (Contains Gluten)

  • Soy Sauce (醤油 Shoyu): Brewed with equal parts wheat and soybeans. Found in almost all pre-seasoned broths, tares, and glazes.
  • Tempura, Katsu & Fried Food (天ぷら / カツ): Coated in wheat flour/panko and fried in shared fryers.
  • Ramen, Udon & Soba (ラーメン / うどん / 蕎麦): Ramen/Udon are pure wheat noodles. Most Soba noodles also blend 20-30% wheat flour unless 100% Juwari (十割). Broths contain wheat soy sauce.
  • Barley Miso & Tea (麦味噌 / 麦茶 Mugi-cha): Barley contains gluten. Request rice/soy miso or green tea (Sencha/Ryokucha).
  • Japanese Curry (カレー): Traditional curry roux is thickened with wheat flour.
  • Okonomiyaki & Takoyaki: Wheat flour batter and Worcestershire/katsu sauces containing gluten.
  • Chikuwa & Kamaboko (練り物): Processed fish cakes usually use wheat starch as a binder.

Coeliac Restaurant Card

シェフ・店員様へのご案内 (Show to staff / chef)

店員様・調理場へ提示

【重要】セリアック病（重度小麦・グルテンアレルギー）があります。

小麦こむぎ、大麦おおむぎ、ライ麦らいむぎを一切摂取できません。

  • 調味料：普通の醤油しょうゆ、たれ、つゆ、麦味噌、だしの素も一切摂取できません。
  • コンタミネーション：揚げ油の共用、同じ茹で汁や調理器具の使い回しも避ける必要があります。

小麦を含まない料理や、塩・素焼きなど調味料なしで提供できるものはありますか？

English Translation

"[Important] I have Coeliac disease (severe wheat/gluten allergy). I cannot consume any wheat, barley, or rye. This includes seasonings such as regular soy sauce, tare sauce, tsuyu, barley miso, and dashi bouillon powder. Cross-contamination from shared frying oil, boiling water, or shared utensils must also be avoided. Are there dishes without wheat, or can plain grilled/salted dishes be prepared?"
